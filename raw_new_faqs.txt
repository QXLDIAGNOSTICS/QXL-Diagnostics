Good to know: A CKD diagnosis requires evidence the kidney function drop has lasted at least 3 months; a urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio is also essential and isn't a blood test.

Anxiety Disorders
Good to know: No blood test diagnoses an anxiety disorder itself — testing is used to exclude medical causes alongside a clinical assessment.

Mental Health
Depression
Good to know: No blood test diagnoses depression. If you're having thoughts of self-harm or feel unable to stay safe, please seek immediate help rather than waiting on test results.

Good to know: A sudden, severe headache, or one with fever, confusion, or focal weakness, needs urgent in-person evaluation, not routine blood testing.
