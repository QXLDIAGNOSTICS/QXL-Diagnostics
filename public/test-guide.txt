Choose the right level of guidance
SELF-REQUEST POSSIBLE
Suitable for selected wellness or monitoring needs. Reception will confirm fasting, specimen and timing requirements. Unexpected results should be reviewed by a clinician.

Symptoms, medicines, age, menstrual-cycle timing or previous reports can change which tests are appropriate. Discuss the package before booking.
DOCTOR-DIRECTED

Before sample collection
• Confirm whether fasting is required; water is usually allowed unless advised otherwise.
• Tell reception about medicines, supplements, pregnancy, cycle day and recent illness.
• Bring previous reports and prescriptions for specialist or monitoring packages.
• Urgent symptoms require immediate medical assessment - not package testing.
