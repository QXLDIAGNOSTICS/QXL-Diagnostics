# QXL DIAGNOSTICS - 100-Keyword SEO Master Plan

## Google Search • AI Discovery • AEO • GEO • Local Search
**Purpose:** Give the QXL website/SEO team a direct implementation map for the 100 highest-priority search territories, while avoiding duplicate thin pages and keyword cannibalization.

### Strategic principle
* 100 keywords should not become 100 separate webpages. Closely related search intents should reinforce one authoritative canonical page.
* Priorities reflect booking intent, Bengaluru relevance, competitive opportunity and QXL's clinical strengths; they are not claimed exact monthly search-volume rankings.
* Use both “Bangalore” and “Bengaluru” naturally: Bangalore for common search phrasing, Bengaluru for official location/entity references.
* Only publish service claims, locations, accreditation statements and turnaround times that are operationally accurate and verifiable.

**Recommended positioning:** QXL Diagnostics — Doctor-Led NABL Accredited Super-Speciality Reference Laboratory in Bengaluru.
