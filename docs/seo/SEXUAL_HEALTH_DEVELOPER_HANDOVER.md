## 2. Priority clinical corrections (existing pages as well)

- Remove the HIV widget's unsupported precision percentages (95–98%, 99%, 100% definitive). Show method-specific usual detection windows: venous lab Ag/Ab 18–45 days, antibody 23–90 days, rapid fingerstick Ag/Ab 18–90 days, NAT 10–33 days in the absence of antiretrovirals. No immediate detection. PEP/PrEP and new exposures affect follow-up. Do not imply a fixed day 90 blood panel rules out every STI. Do not call a second screening immunoassay confirmatory unless its use in the applicable diagnostic algorithm is verified.
- Urgent PEP assessment within 72 hours; do not wait for testing. Emergency contraception, hepatitis B prophylaxis and sexual assault require time-sensitive clinician support. Do not present routine booking as emergency medical care. Avoid absolute reassurance or test-of-cure dates unrelated to the actual infection.
- Avoid indiscriminate HSV IgG screening, HSV IgM, broad asymptomatic Mycoplasma/Ureaplasma screening or universal HPV testing. Routine pharyngeal chlamydia screening is generally not recommended; dual-target assays may report CT. Do not imply negative urine excludes throat or rectal infection. Respect anatomy, exposure and clinician-directed testing.
- Replace absolute anonymity/confidentiality claims with verified policy. Clearly explain applicable identification, consent, reporting and confidentiality exceptions. Do not use “100% private” or “100% accurate.”
