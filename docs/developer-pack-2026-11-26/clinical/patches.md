# Clinical correction patch — draft for medical sign-off
Apply to page text, FAQs, metadata, JSON-LD, internal search, public catalogue and AI files; no blanket string substitutions. Review the source record once and regenerate all outputs. Preserve canonical URLs. Do not mark reviewed by a named doctor until actual approval is recorded.

## /sexual-health/sti-testing-window-periods and /health-hub/hiv-test-window-period
Replace absolute HIV conclusiveness and universal 90-day statements with:
> A laboratory HIV antigen/antibody test on venous blood usually detects infection 18–45 days after exposure. Other tests have different detection windows. A negative result obtained before the applicable window ends may need repeat testing. PEP, PrEP and other antiretroviral use can alter detection; follow the clinician-directed testing schedule. Reactive screening results require confirmation using the applicable national diagnostic algorithm. If a possible exposure occurred within the last 72 hours, seek urgent medical assessment for PEP without waiting for test results.
Evidence: https://www.cdc.gov/hiv/testing/ ; https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/74/rr/rr7401a1.htm . QXL Medical Director must reconcile with current applicable Indian/NACO protocol before publication. CDC 2025 nPEP follow-up includes Ag/Ab plus NAT at specified follow-ups; do not offer a universal automated date.

## Food IgG panel (homepage, packages, test pages)
> Food-specific IgG is not a validated diagnostic test for food allergy, intolerance or sensitivity. It can reflect exposure to foods and should not be used alone to justify dietary restriction. For suspected food allergy or intolerance, seek appropriate clinical assessment.
Remove diagnostic sales claims and automatic elimination-diet recommendations. Source: https://www.aaaai.org/tools-for-the-public/conditions-library/allergies/igg-food-test

## Tumour markers / pancreatic content
> Tumour markers may assist selected clinical investigations or monitoring in defined contexts. They do not independently establish or exclude cancer and most are not appropriate for general-population screening. The treating clinician determines whether imaging, histopathology or additional investigations are indicated.
Remove cancer-detection guarantees and automatic broad marker screening recommendations. Approve indication-specific references and pathways separately.

## Bone, inflammation and cardiovascular content
Bone biomarkers do not replace indicated DXA. Calprotectin thresholds and predictive values vary by assay and patient population. hs-CRP is nonspecific; acute illness can affect interpretation. NT-proBNP and troponin are not routine screening score inputs; acute symptoms require urgent clinical assessment. Lp(a) mass and molar concentrations must not be converted using a universal factor. Separate reference intervals from clinical decision limits.
