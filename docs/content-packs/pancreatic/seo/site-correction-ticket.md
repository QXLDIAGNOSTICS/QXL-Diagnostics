# Developer tickets for current QXL live content

**Priority P0 — medical accuracy:** `https://www.qxldiagnostics.com/specialities/gastroenterology` currently advertises a “Pancreatic Wellness Panel” (amylase, lipase, fasting blood sugar, HbA1c, optional CA 19-9). Update the heading and copy to:

> **Pancreatic and Digestive Laboratory Investigations (Doctor-Selected)**. Serum lipase and amylase may assist in evaluating pancreatitis; glucose/HbA1c assess glycaemic status; stool elastase assesses suspected exocrine insufficiency; and CA 19-9 is used only when requested for appropriate specialist oncological management. These tests do not screen for, diagnose or exclude pancreatic cancer. Red-flag symptoms require medical evaluation and often pancreatic/biliary imaging.

**Priority P0 — tumour marker messaging:** `https://www.qxldiagnostics.com/specialities/oncology` currently lists male/female tumour marker panels and a “GI oncology panel”. Review copy and checkout warnings; **do not present multi-tumour-marker panels as effective population cancer screening**, especially CA 19-9. Preserve valid clinician-directed testing and disease monitoring.

**Priority P0 — emergency triage:** Add call center script and website non-emergency CTA: **“New jaundice, jaundice with fever, severe pain or sudden shortness of breath? Seek urgent/emergency care; please do not delay for a home sample collection.”**

**Priority P1 — pathways:** Link the flagship clinically appropriate content from both service pages and the blog; provide physician-prescribed testing support rather than a presumed cancer diagnosis.

**Priority P1 — QXL scope:** QXL being NABL accredited does **not** mean every test on the menu is individually NABL accredited. Test-level claims require matching the current certificate scope.
