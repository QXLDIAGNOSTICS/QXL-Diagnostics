## Editorial vs operational approval
The medical wording is marked reviewed and authorised by the named physician based on the explicit authorisation provided for this commission. It is not evidence that live test availability, processing platforms, Same Day TAT or package prices were operationally checked. Those are separate laboratory service approval gates.

## Medical red-flag review
Check that each page communicates the limitations of the test and urgency of significant symptoms. No CA-125 or PSA test is promoted as population cancer exclusion. ANCA positives are not diagnostic; negative NAAT cannot exclude TB.

## Analytical validation gate
For every assay, verify test code, tube/container, specimen matrix, platform/reagent, detection limits/cutoffs if quoted, method interference notes, sample stability and whether QXL processes it in-house or as referral. Confirm the specific accreditation scope under MC-6849.

## Same Day handling
The requested label is Same Day*. Publish the asterisk definition adjacent to each claim: suitable specimen, receipt before cut-off, assay schedule and operational confirmation. Do not promise same day for TB extrapulmonary samples or extended reflex testing unless confirmed.

## Pricing and packages
All seven named bundles are concepts, not confirmed booking SKUs. Have the clinical service director approve appropriate indications and optional panels, operations verify specimens/TAT, quality verify accreditation wording, and commercial team approve price before booking activation.

## SEO governance
Inspect current indexed URLs first. Publish canonical server-rendered articles. Validate Search Console indexing, Bing Webmaster Tools, structured data visible FAQs, reviewer provenance and internal linking. Rank #1 is an objective, not a verifiable claim or guarantee.

## AI-search governance
Use answer-first summaries, explicit citations, precise medical caveats, public stable URLs, text crawlability, and factual publisher/reviewer details. Measure AI result appearances with repeatable prompts but do not falsely claim citations are assured.
