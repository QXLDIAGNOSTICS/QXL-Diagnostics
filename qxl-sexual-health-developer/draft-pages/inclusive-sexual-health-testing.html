Quick clinical answer

Sexual health testing should be respectful and based on anatomy, exposure sites, symptoms and individual risk, not assumptions about sexual orientation, gender identity, marital status or occupation.

What to do next

People with new or multiple partners, LGBTQ+ people, students and unmarried couples may request a private discussion of suitable tests. Confirm the laboratory’s confidentiality and collection policies; arrange clinician-directed screening where appropriate.

Frequently asked questions

Can I request testing regardless of gender or relationship status?

Discuss individualized testing confidentially; applicable consent and identification requirements still apply.

What is site-specific testing for men who have sex with men?

Depending on exposure, urethral, rectal and pharyngeal screening may be indicated; clinician guidance determines timing.

Can testing be non-judgmental and discreet?

You may request a private enquiry, ask about report delivery and confirm confidentiality practices before booking.
