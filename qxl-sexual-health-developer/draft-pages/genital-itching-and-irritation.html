Private-Part Itching, Redness, Burning and Genital Skin Irritation

Possible HIV exposure within 72 hours? Seek urgent medical assessment for PEP now; do not wait for a laboratory test. Severe pelvic or testicular pain, heavy bleeding, breathing difficulty or sexual assault may need immediate medical care. Read urgent-care guidance.

Quick clinical answer

Genital itching, redness, swelling, cracked skin and peeling may reflect fungal infection, dermatitis, balanitis, irritation or an STI. Symptoms after sex do not prove transmission.

What to do next

Seek a clinician’s examination. Depending on findings, microscopy, culture or targeted molecular testing may be useful. Avoid applying harsh chemicals or self-medicating.

Frequently asked questions

Is itching after sex always an STD?

No. Irritants, yeast and other skin conditions are common alternative causes.

Can red or white spots on the foreskin be diagnosed from a photo?

Appearance alone is often insufficient; examination and selected testing may be necessary.

Does a blood STI panel diagnose balanitis?

Not by itself. Local examination and specimen testing may be needed.
