Negative STI Tests But Symptoms Persist – Next Steps

Possible HIV exposure within 72 hours? Seek urgent medical assessment for PEP now; do not wait for a laboratory test. Severe pelvic or testicular pain, heavy bleeding, breathing difficulty or sexual assault may need immediate medical care. Read urgent-care guidance.

Quick clinical answer

A negative result answers a particular question for a specific specimen, method and time. It may not exclude infection at another site, a condition not included in the panel, or non-infectious causes.

What to do next

Review the exact report and exposure with a clinician rather than repeating broad panels indefinitely. Persistent genital pain, discharge or lesions warrant examination.

Frequently asked questions

Can a urine PCR miss a throat or rectal infection?

Yes. Anatomical site is important.

Can every genital symptom be caused by an STI?

No. Dermatological, urinary and other conditions can have similar symptoms.

Do I need repeated negative HIV tests?

Review the window and any new exposure or antiretroviral use with a clinician; unnecessary repeats may worsen anxiety.
