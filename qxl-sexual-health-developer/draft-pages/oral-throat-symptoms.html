Sore Throat, Mouth Ulcers or White Tonsil Spots After Oral Sex

Possible HIV exposure within 72 hours? Seek urgent medical assessment for PEP now; do not wait for a laboratory test. Severe pelvic or testicular pain, heavy bleeding, breathing difficulty or sexual assault may need immediate medical care. Read urgent-care guidance.

Quick clinical answer

Sore throat, white patches, swollen glands, mouth sores and painful swallowing after oral sex have many possible causes, including common respiratory infections. A throat STI may also be asymptomatic.

What to do next

Seek examination for persistent symptoms. A site-specific gonorrhoea NAAT may be appropriate after pharyngeal exposure. A regular throat culture is not automatically equivalent to STI NAAT. Difficulty breathing or swallowing saliva needs urgent care.

Frequently asked questions

Can gonorrhoea look like tonsillitis?

Symptoms overlap; appropriate testing is needed.

Can negative urine PCR exclude throat infection?

No. Specimen site matters.

Is every sore throat after oral sex an STI?

No. Common throat infections and non-infectious causes are possible.
