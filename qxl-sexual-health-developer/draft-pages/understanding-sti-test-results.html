Understanding STI Reports – Reactive, Non-Reactive, Detected and Not Detected

Possible HIV exposure within 72 hours? Seek urgent medical assessment for PEP now; do not wait for a laboratory test. Severe pelvic or testicular pain, heavy bleeding, breathing difficulty or sexual assault may need immediate medical care. Read urgent-care guidance.

Quick clinical answer

Screening results, quantitative titres, antibody index values and molecular Ct values have different meanings. Interpretation depends on the assay and clinical history.

What to do next

A reactive HIV screening result is not a confirmed diagnosis. A positive treponemal result may reflect treated syphilis. HCV antibody requires RNA assessment for current infection. A laboratory report should be reviewed with a clinician.

Frequently asked questions

What is HIV S/CO or COI value?

It is an assay-specific signal or index used against its cutoff; it is not a viral load or measure of infection risk.

Does PCR Ct tell how infectious I am?

Not reliably by itself; Ct depends on assay and specimen, and should not be used as a standalone measure of infectiousness.

What does not detected mean?

The target was not detected in the tested specimen under assay conditions; timing and specimen quality matter.

Should I repeat an unexpected reactive result?

Follow the recommended supplemental testing algorithm with a clinician.
