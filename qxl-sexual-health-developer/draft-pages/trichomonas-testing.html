Quick clinical answer

Trichomoniasis can cause vaginal discharge or urethral symptoms and may be asymptomatic. Suitable molecular testing can detect the organism in approved specimens.

What to do next

QXL lists a CT/NG/Trichomonas molecular profile. Confirm specimen suitability, particularly for male urine, before collection. A clinician determines indications and follow-up.

Frequently asked questions

Does frothy discharge prove trichomoniasis?

No. Symptoms overlap with other vaginal infections.

Can urine routine detect trichomonas?

Routine microscopy is not a reliable substitute for appropriate targeted testing.

Can one PCR detect three pathogens?

Some multiplex assays detect chlamydia, gonorrhoea and trichomonas; confirm the platform and sample type.
