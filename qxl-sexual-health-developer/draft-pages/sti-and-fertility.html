STIs, Fertility and Reproductive Health Investigations

Possible HIV exposure within 72 hours? Seek urgent medical assessment for PEP now; do not wait for a laboratory test. Severe pelvic or testicular pain, heavy bleeding, breathing difficulty or sexual assault may need immediate medical care. Read urgent-care guidance.

Quick clinical answer

Untreated chlamydia and gonorrhoea can contribute to pelvic inflammatory disease and fertility problems. Genital symptoms or infertility have many other causes.

What to do next

Seek appropriate gynecology, urology or fertility assessment. STI NAAT, semen testing and other investigations answer different questions and are chosen clinically.

Frequently asked questions

Does a negative STI test rule out past complications?

No. A current negative infection test cannot exclude a prior cleared infection or resulting damage.

Can a semen culture diagnose every STI?

No. Targeted specimen-specific methods may be needed.

What about screening before IVF?

Follow the fertility centre’s clinical and regulatory requirements.
