STI Testing After Antibiotics, PEP or Previous Treatment

Possible HIV exposure within 72 hours? Seek urgent medical assessment for PEP now; do not wait for a laboratory test. Severe pelvic or testicular pain, heavy bleeding, breathing difficulty or sexual assault may need immediate medical care. Read urgent-care guidance.

Quick clinical answer

Antibiotics can alter organism detection and residual nucleic acid can sometimes remain after treatment. HIV prevention medication can influence HIV detection windows.

What to do next

Tell the clinician the actual medicine, dates and doses. Distinguish test of cure from retesting for reinfection; avoid self-medication and unscheduled repeat testing.

Frequently asked questions

Can antibiotics make STI PCR negative?

Treatment can reduce detectable organisms; interpretation depends on timing and organism.

Can PCR stay positive after treatment?

Yes, some NAATs may detect residual nucleic acid; follow pathogen-specific guidance.

Can both partners need evaluation?

Yes. Partner assessment may be necessary to prevent reinfection.
