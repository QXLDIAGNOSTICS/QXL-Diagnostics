Genital Sores, Blisters, Boils, Marks and Ulcers After Sex

Possible HIV exposure within 72 hours? Seek urgent medical assessment for PEP now; do not wait for a laboratory test. Severe pelvic or testicular pain, heavy bleeding, breathing difficulty or sexual assault may need immediate medical care. Read urgent-care guidance.

Quick clinical answer

Painful blisters, a painless ulcer, cuts, pimples, boils, scars or warts can have multiple infectious and non-infectious causes. A photograph or pain pattern does not establish a diagnosis.

What to do next

A fresh lesion may be suitable for HSV PCR; syphilis serology and selected lesion testing may be indicated. Assess persistent or worsening lesions promptly; clinical examination is important.

Frequently asked questions

Is a painless genital sore always syphilis?

No. Syphilis is one possibility and requires appropriate clinical and laboratory assessment.

Which test detects herpes from an active blister?

A lesion NAAT/PCR is preferred when a suitable active lesion is present.

Can a genital wart be diagnosed with a blood HPV test?

No routine blood HPV test establishes a wart diagnosis. Seek clinical assessment.
