STI Risk From Kissing, Genital Rubbing and Non-Penetrative Sex

Possible HIV exposure within 72 hours? Seek urgent medical assessment for PEP now; do not wait for a laboratory test. Severe pelvic or testicular pain, heavy bleeding, breathing difficulty or sexual assault may need immediate medical care. Read urgent-care guidance.

Quick clinical answer

STI risk depends on the contact. HIV does not spread through casual touching or saliva; ordinary kissing does not transmit HIV. Herpes, HPV and some other infections can spread through direct contact with infected skin or lesions.

What to do next

Do not assume every intimate activity requires a full STI panel. Discuss any actual blood exposure, open sores, shared sex toys or mucosal contact with a clinician. Avoid sharing sex toys without appropriate cleaning and protection.

Frequently asked questions

Can kissing transmit HIV?

Ordinary kissing does not transmit HIV. Seek advice for unusual blood exposure rather than routine testing for kissing alone.

Can an STI spread without penetration or ejaculation?

Yes, certain infections spread through skin or mucosal contact.

Does touching semen or genital fluids require an HIV test?

Intact skin contact does not transmit HIV. A clinician can assess contact with mucosa or damaged skin if relevant.
