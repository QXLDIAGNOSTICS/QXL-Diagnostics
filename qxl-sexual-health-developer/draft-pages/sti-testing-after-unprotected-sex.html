STI Testing After Unprotected Sex in Bangalore

Possible HIV exposure within 72 hours? Seek urgent medical assessment for PEP now; do not wait for a laboratory test. Severe pelvic or testicular pain, heavy bleeding, breathing difficulty or sexual assault may need immediate medical care. Read urgent-care guidance.

Quick clinical answer

Testing after sex without a condom depends on the type of exposure, anatomical sites, symptoms, timing, vaccination and treatment history. Blood tests may assess HIV, syphilis and viral hepatitis; site-specific NAAT may detect gonorrhoea and chlamydia.

What to do next

If possible HIV exposure occurred within 72 hours, seek urgent medical assessment for PEP. Do not wait for testing. If pregnancy is possible, seek timely emergency contraception advice. A clinician can decide whether baseline and follow-up testing are appropriate.

Frequently asked questions

What should I do after unprotected sex yesterday?

Seek urgent medical advice for possible HIV PEP within 72 hours and pregnancy prevention when relevant; baseline tests do not exclude the recent exposure.

Can one sexual encounter cause an STI?

Yes, risk varies considerably by infection, contact and partner status. Symptoms alone cannot establish infection.

Which tests should I get after sex without a condom?

Depending on risk, consider HIV Ag/Ab, syphilis serology, HBV/HCV assessment and site-specific gonorrhoea/chlamydia NAAT.

Does washing or urinating after sex prevent STIs?

No. Washing or urinating does not reliably prevent STIs. Avoid irritating chemicals and unprescribed antibiotics.
