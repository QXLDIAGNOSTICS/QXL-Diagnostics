Other Genital Infections – Candida, BV, Dermatitis and Less Common Causes

Possible HIV exposure within 72 hours? Seek urgent medical assessment for PEP now; do not wait for a laboratory test. Severe pelvic or testicular pain, heavy bleeding, breathing difficulty or sexual assault may need immediate medical care. Read urgent-care guidance.

Quick clinical answer

Genital symptoms are not synonymous with STIs. Candida, bacterial vaginosis, dermatitis, scabies and other conditions may cause itching or lesions. Less common infections such as LGV, chancroid, mpox or donovanosis may require specialist evaluation.

What to do next

Target testing to clinical findings, anatomy and epidemiology. Confirm whether specific cultures or molecular assays are available; do not assume every organism is included in an STI package.

Frequently asked questions

Does Candida always mean sexual transmission?

No. Yeast infections can occur without sexual transmission.

Can genital boils be non-sexually transmitted?

Yes. Folliculitis and other skin conditions are possible.

Can a broad multiplex result diagnose every genital symptom?

No. Examination and clinical context remain essential.
