Couples, Premarital and New-Partner STI Screening in Bangalore

Possible HIV exposure within 72 hours? Seek urgent medical assessment for PEP now; do not wait for a laboratory test. Severe pelvic or testicular pain, heavy bleeding, breathing difficulty or sexual assault may need immediate medical care. Read urgent-care guidance.

Quick clinical answer

Couples may choose risk-based screening before a new relationship or pregnancy. Results depend on each person’s exposure and timing, and one partner’s result does not establish the other’s status.

What to do next

Discuss consent, vaccination, test selection and window periods. If a partner has a confirmed STI, seek clinician assessment; partner management may be needed.

Frequently asked questions

Can one partner be positive and the other negative?

Yes. Transmission and detection vary.

Can I ask for my partner’s report?

Reports are confidential and should be shared only with appropriate authorization and legal requirements.

Is every STI panel necessary before marriage?

No. Select tests through risk assessment and shared decision-making.
