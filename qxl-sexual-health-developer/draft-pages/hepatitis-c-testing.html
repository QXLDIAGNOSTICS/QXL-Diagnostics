Hepatitis C Antibody and HCV RNA PCR After Sexual Exposure

Possible HIV exposure within 72 hours? Seek urgent medical assessment for PEP now; do not wait for a laboratory test. Severe pelvic or testicular pain, heavy bleeding, breathing difficulty or sexual assault may need immediate medical care. Read urgent-care guidance.

Quick clinical answer

Hepatitis C is primarily transmitted through blood; sexual transmission risk varies by circumstances. An HCV antibody test detects exposure, and HCV RNA testing assesses current infection.

What to do next

A reactive antibody result requires RNA testing to determine current infection. Recent exposure can warrant RNA testing or follow-up despite negative antibodies.

Frequently asked questions

Can HCV antibody be positive with RNA not detected?

Yes; this can reflect resolved infection or a false-reactive antibody result.

Can I get hepatitis C through sexual contact?

It is possible, particularly where blood exposure or specific risk factors are present.

Which HCV test is appropriate after recent exposure?

A clinician may select RNA testing and follow-up based on timing and risk.
