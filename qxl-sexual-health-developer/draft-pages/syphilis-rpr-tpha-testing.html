Quick clinical answer

Syphilis serology generally uses a non-treponemal test such as RPR or VDRL together with a treponemal test such as TPHA. Results require clinical context and previous treatment history.

What to do next

A quantitative RPR titre may assist follow-up; early disease can be seronegative. Examination of sores or unexplained rash is important.

Frequently asked questions

What does RPR reactive or a titre of 1:4 mean?

A titre is not a standalone diagnosis. Interpret with treponemal results, history and clinician assessment.

Why is TPHA positive but RPR negative?

Possible explanations include previously treated infection, early infection or other patterns requiring review.

Do treponemal tests remain positive after cure?

Often yes; they are not generally used to monitor treatment response.
