Pregnancy Testing and STI Screening After Unprotected Sex

Possible HIV exposure within 72 hours? Seek urgent medical assessment for PEP now; do not wait for a laboratory test. Severe pelvic or testicular pain, heavy bleeding, breathing difficulty or sexual assault may need immediate medical care. Read urgent-care guidance.

Quick clinical answer

Pregnancy and STI risk are distinct. If pregnancy is possible after recent unprotected intercourse, emergency contraception advice is time-sensitive. A urine or serum hCG test may be useful at appropriate timing.

What to do next

Seek antenatal guidance for HIV, syphilis, hepatitis B and other indicated testing according to local protocols. Severe pelvic pain or pregnancy with bleeding needs urgent evaluation.

Frequently asked questions

Can I test for pregnancy seven days after sex?

A test this early may miss pregnancy. Follow clinician or test instructions and repeat if appropriate.

Can emergency contraception prevent STIs?

No. It does not prevent infection.

Which STI tests are appropriate during pregnancy?

Antenatal protocols and individual risk determine tests; discuss with an obstetric clinician.
