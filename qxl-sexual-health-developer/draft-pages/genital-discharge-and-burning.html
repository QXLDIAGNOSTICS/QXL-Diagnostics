Penile Discharge, Burning Urine and Private-Part Pain After Sex

Possible HIV exposure within 72 hours? Seek urgent medical assessment for PEP now; do not wait for a laboratory test. Severe pelvic or testicular pain, heavy bleeding, breathing difficulty or sexual assault may need immediate medical care. Read urgent-care guidance.

Quick clinical answer

Clear, white, yellow or green discharge, urethral burning and pain with urination can occur with urethritis or other conditions including urinary infection. Color alone does not identify the cause.

What to do next

Clinical assessment may include first-catch urine or suitable genital swab NAAT for gonorrhoea/chlamydia, urine culture where indicated and other tests for persistent symptoms. Severe testicular pain or swelling requires urgent care.

Frequently asked questions

Is watery penile leakage always an STI?

No. It may have infectious or non-infectious causes; assessment is more reliable than appearance.

Can a normal urine routine rule out gonorrhoea?

No. Targeted NAAT is different from urinalysis.

What about persistent discharge after antibiotics?

Seek reassessment, including possible reinfection, resistance, Mycoplasma genitalium or non-infectious causes.
