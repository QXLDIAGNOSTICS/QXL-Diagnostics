UTI vs STI – Burning Urine, Urethritis and Urine PCR Testing

Possible HIV exposure within 72 hours? Seek urgent medical assessment for PEP now; do not wait for a laboratory test. Severe pelvic or testicular pain, heavy bleeding, breathing difficulty or sexual assault may need immediate medical care. Read urgent-care guidance.

Quick clinical answer

Painful or frequent urination can be caused by UTI, STI-related urethritis or other conditions. Routine urine analysis and midstream urine culture address different questions from a first-catch urine STI NAAT.

What to do next

A clinician may order both types of testing depending on symptoms. Follow collection instructions because midstream and first-catch specimens are not interchangeable.

Frequently asked questions

Does urine culture detect chlamydia?

Routine urine culture does not reliably detect chlamydia; use an appropriate NAAT.

Why is urine culture negative but burning persists?

Possible causes include urethritis, prior treatment, irritation or other urinary disorders.

Can I submit one urine sample for every test?

Not necessarily. Ask the laboratory about separate containers and collection methods.
