Hepatitis B Triple Panel – HBsAg, Anti-HBs and Total Anti-HBc

Possible HIV exposure within 72 hours? Seek urgent medical assessment for PEP now; do not wait for a laboratory test. Severe pelvic or testicular pain, heavy bleeding, breathing difficulty or sexual assault may need immediate medical care. Read urgent-care guidance.

Quick clinical answer

The HBV triple panel comprises hepatitis B surface antigen, surface antibody and total core antibody. The combination helps distinguish infection, immunity and susceptibility. HBsAg alone is not the triple panel.

What to do next

Confirm the availability and fee for all three components with QXL. Vaccination, exposure timing and additional tests may influence interpretation. A clinician can assess hepatitis B vaccine or immunoglobulin needs after relevant recent exposure.

Frequently asked questions

Is total anti-HBc the same as IgM anti-HBc?

No. Total anti-HBc measures total core antibodies; IgM is a separate marker used in selected clinical situations.

Can I check hepatitis B immunity after vaccination?

Anti-HBs may help in indicated circumstances; interpret according to vaccination history and clinical context.

Do I need HBV DNA PCR after exposure?

Not routinely for everyone. It may be appropriate for selected diagnosis or management questions.
