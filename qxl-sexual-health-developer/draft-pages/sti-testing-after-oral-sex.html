STI Screening After Oral Sex – Throat Swab PCR & Comprehensive Blood Testing

Possible HIV exposure within 72 hours? Seek urgent medical assessment for PEP now; do not wait for a laboratory test. Severe pelvic or testicular pain, heavy bleeding, breathing difficulty or sexual assault may need immediate medical care. Read urgent-care guidance.

Quick clinical answer

Oral exposure can transmit gonorrhoea, syphilis, herpes and other infections. HIV risk from oral sex alone is little to none. Throat gonorrhoea NAAT may be appropriate after pharyngeal exposure; some suitable dual-target assays also detect chlamydia.

What to do next

Urine cannot exclude an infection confined to the throat. Routine pharyngeal chlamydia screening is generally not recommended. QXL lists extragenital screening; confirm the kit, targets, validated specimen, performing lab, collection location, fee and turnaround before collection. Blood and genital tests should be selected separately according to risk.

Frequently asked questions

Can a throat swab test detect both gonorrhoea and chlamydia?

Some dual-target NAATs can detect both in a suitable pharyngeal sample. Ask QXL to confirm the exact assay and verification status.

Is the assay validated for throat swabs?

Ask for the specific platform, manufacturer specimen claim and laboratory verification. A urine-only assay cannot be assumed suitable.

Should I test after oral sex without symptoms?

Testing depends on which site was exposed and individual risk; infections may be asymptomatic.

Can I arrange HIV, RPR/TPHA, hepatitis B triple panel and throat PCR together?

Request an itemised quote and specimen-specific confirmation. HBsAg alone is not the triple panel; genital NAAT is selected when appropriate.

Can I get tested after 3, 6 or 8 months?

Yes, but a negative NAAT months later cannot prove an infection never occurred.
